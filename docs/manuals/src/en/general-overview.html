What is Televoto

Televoto is a web platform designed for organizers running events with live voting: talent shows, artistic or sports competitions, hackathons, festivals, corporate or school contests. It replaces paper ballots, spreadsheets, and manual counting with a real-time digital system, accessible from a smartphone via a simple link or QR code — no app to download, no account to create for voters.

How it works, in 3 phases

1
⚙️
Set up the event

The organizer creates the event, adds the candidates/competitors, and generates the access codes for judges and the public.

2
📲
Live voting

Judges and the public receive a link or a QR code, open the page on their phone, and assign a score to each candidate.

3
🏆
Ranking and reveal

The final score is calculated automatically and revealed with a dedicated scenic effect: the Final Ranking.

Key features

⚖️
Dual voting channel

Qualified jury (weighted vote) and general public, combined with customizable weights per event (e.g. 70% jury / 30% public).

🔐
Single-use voting codes

Every voter receives a unique code shared via link or QR, valid for one vote per candidate: no duplicate votes.

📡
Live dashboard

The organizer sees in real time how many codes have been used and how voting is progressing, without needing to reload the page.

🗂
Multi-event management

A central panel to organize multiple events, each with its own candidates, codes, and password, fully isolated from one another.

📈
Configurable scoring

Weighted average between jury and public, with an optional "trimmed mean" to reduce the impact of anomalous or outlier votes.

🎉
Final Ranking

A final-ranking reveal screen designed for the event's key moment, in front of the audience.

Who it's for

Televoto adapts to any live event where a vote needs to be collected from multiple people, quickly and transparently:

🎤 Talent shows 🏅 Competitions and contests 💻 Hackathons 🎭 Festivals and cultural events 🏢 Corporate events 🏫 School contests 🎶 Music contests 🍕 Fairs and fêtes
Zero friction for voters

Just open a link or scan a QR code from your phone. No registration, no personal data required, no app to install.

Security and reliability

🔑
Separate access levels

Dedicated password for the general administrator and for each individual event organizer.

🎟
Protected codes

Voting codes are opaque and single-use, stored only in encrypted form: never in plain text in the system.

🕵
No tracking

Voters are not profiled: no personal data or device identifier is collected.
