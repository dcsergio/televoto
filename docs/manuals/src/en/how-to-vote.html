How to vote — Quick guide

📱 TELEVOTO
  1. 1

    🔗 Get your code

    The organizer gives you a QR code or a personal link. It's your voting code, for one-time use.

    ⭐ Judges (QUALIFICATA) 👥 Public (POPOLARE)

  2. 2

    📷 Open and enter

    Scan the QR code with your camera, or go to the site and enter the event code.

    Just one tap: no registration, no password.

  3. 3

    🔢 Enter your voting code

    16 characters, split into 4 groups of 4. If you open the link, it's already pre-filled.

    A1B2 C3D4 E5F6 G7H8

  4. 4

    🎯 Choose and vote

    Tap a candidate, then give a score from 1 to 10 with a tap.

    1 3 5 7 9 10

  5. 5

    📊 Vote for every candidate

    One vote per candidate, not editable after submitting. A progress bar shows how many votes are still missing.

    The page updates itself: if voting closes, your vote is locked.

  6. 6

    ✅ Final confirmation

    When you're done, press "Final confirmation": the code is locked and your vote becomes official.

    Done! Thanks for voting.

Warning

If voting is shown as closed, you can no longer vote — try again later or contact the event staff.
